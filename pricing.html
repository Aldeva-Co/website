Pricing questions

Is there a setup fee?

Most local businesses: no. Growing companies have a one-time setup that covers connections, clean-up and the parallel close.

What if I'm behind on my books?

Catch-up is quoted after your free review and usually folded into setup.

What if I want to leave?

Your books are already in your own software, so you keep everything — history, reports, and our notes. We help with the hand-off.

Are payroll and tax filing extra?

No separate payroll company needed. For Florida employers we run payroll in-house: paychecks by direct deposit, tax deposits paid from your own account, quarterly and year-end filings, W-2s and 1099s. It is included in the plans that list payroll. Business tax returns are quoted separately unless your plan includes them.
