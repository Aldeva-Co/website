Privacy policy

Last updated October 2026.

Website analytics and advertising

Our website may use cookies and similar tools from Google, Meta and visitor-analytics providers to measure visits and to show our ads to people who have visited this site. You can opt out of personalized ads in your Google and Meta ad settings, or block cookies in your browser.

What we collect

When you request a free books review we collect the details you enter: your name, business email, phone number, business type, accounting software, revenue range, and any note you add. If you become a client, we access your accounting and bank data only to provide the services you sign up for.

How we use it

To contact you about your review, prepare your report, and deliver our services. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not use your financial data for advertising.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers we need to run the business (for example email, hosting, and secure bank and accounting connections), under confidentiality obligations — or where the law requires it.

Security

Bank and accounting connections use their official secure connection services; we never ask for or store your banking passwords. Access to client data is limited to the people working on your account.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct, or delete the information you gave us, or to stop contacting you. Call (647) 521-9929 or reply to any email from us.
