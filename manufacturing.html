What we handle

Cost accounting a plant controller would sign.

The costing, inventory and margin work that sits between production and the financial statements.

  • Standard costing and variancesMaterial price and usage, labor rate and efficiency, and overhead spending and volume variances, measured and explained every month.
  • Job order or process costingJob costing for custom and batch work, process costing for continuous lines, or a mix, matched to how you produce.
  • Activity-based costingOverhead assigned by what drives it: setups, machine hours, inspections, material handling.

Questions

Straight answers.

We use QuickBooks, not an ERP. Can we still do standard costing?

Yes. Many manufacturers your size keep costing in a controlled schedule alongside the ledger and post summary entries each month. When you outgrow that, we help plan the move to a system.

Job costing or process costing: which do we need?

It depends on how you produce. Custom or batch work usually suits job costing; continuous, repetitive lines suit process costing. Many plants use both. We map your floor first, then choose.

How do you handle the year-end physical count?

We plan the count, set cut-off rules, reconcile the count to the books and explain the differences, so the adjustment is understood, not just booked.

Will you change how our plant records production?

Only where data is missing. We start from the reports you already have and ask for changes only when they make costs materially more accurate.
