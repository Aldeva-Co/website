Questions finance teams ask.

Does Aldeva replace our controller?

No — it removes the data entry and the close grind so your controller can run finance. If you don't have one, our CPA layer covers the review and sign-off.

Do you work inside our books?

Yes. Everything posts into your existing accounting system with a note on why it was posted. Nothing lives in a separate ledger.

How do you handle accruals and month-end entries?

Recurring accruals, prepaids and month-end entries are scheduled and booked as part of the close checklist, then reviewed before sign-off.

What about audit trail and reversals?

Every automatic entry is tagged with its source and reason, logged, and reversible as a batch.

Is my financial data safe?

Yes. Bank connections use the same secure, bank-approved services that most finance apps use, and we never see or store your bank passwords. Your books stay in your own accounting software. See our security page.
