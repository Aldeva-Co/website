Questions

Straight answers.

We already have a bookkeeper. Where does a controller fit?

Your bookkeeper records the transactions. The controller layer reviews them, books the month-end entries, reconciles the balance sheet, runs the controls and signs off. Many clients keep their bookkeeper and add this layer.

Can you get us ready for our first audit?

Yes. We set up the schedules, reconciliations and support an auditor expects, and work through their request list with you.

How long until our books are current every day?

It is built during onboarding. Growing companies are typically live in 6–8 weeks, including a parallel close with your team.

Will you write our accounting policies?

Yes. We document the policies and procedures that fit your business, such as revenue, capitalization, accruals and approvals, and keep them current.
