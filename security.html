Security & privacy

Your money. Your books. Your control.

How we keep your data safe — in plain English.

No passwords shared

Banks connect through secure, bank-approved connection services. Your accounting software connects through its official app connection. We never see or store your logins.

Your books stay in your software

We post into your accounting system. Nothing important lives only with us.

Every entry explained

Each automatic entry is tagged with why it was posted, logged, and can be reversed in one step.

We never hold your money

Payments go from your bank through the provider you choose. Aldeva prepares; you approve.

People and AI, clearly labeled

We tell you where automation is used. A CPA reviews and signs every month.

Only what's needed

We only request the access needed to do your books, and you can disconnect at any time.

Your books, done for you. Every transaction posted automatically, every month closed and signed by a CPA.
