The Florida small-business month-end checklist

What has to be true before a month's books can be trusted. Print it, tick it, or let us do it for you.

Bank & cards

  • Every bank and card account matched to its statement, to the cent
  • No uncategorized or 'Ask my accountant' items left
  • Transfers between your own accounts booked as transfers, not income or expense

Money in

  • Every customer payment matched to its invoice
  • Overdue invoices listed and chased (30 / 60 / 90 days)
  • Deposits that pay several invoices split correctly

Money out

  • Every bill entered once, and paid bills marked paid
  • Card-paid bills not also entered as a bill (no double counting)
  • Receipts attached for anything over $75

Payroll & tax

  • Payroll journal matches the payroll report
  • Florida sales tax collected = sales tax filed (if you sell taxable items)
  • 1099 contractors have a W-9 on file

Close the month

  • Prepaid and annual costs spread over the months they cover
  • Profit & loss reviewed against last month: anything odd explained
  • Balance sheet accounts each backed by a statement or schedule
  • Month locked so old numbers can't change by accident

Look ahead

  • Cash for the next 4–8 weeks: payroll, rent, tax, big bills
  • Who owes you and when you'll actually get paid
