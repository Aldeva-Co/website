The controller work, without the full-time hire.

When you have more than one company, location or bank, the books get harder to trust. We run the controller side: a set close calendar, the checks and controls, and numbers your lender or auditor can rely on.

  • Multi-entity and multi-locationCompanies, locations and classes set up properly and kept consistent.
  • Consolidations and intercompanyIntercompany balances matched and eliminated, one consolidated view each month.
  • Month-end close calendarWho does what, by which day, so the close finishes on time every month.
  • Internal controlsApproval limits, separation of duties and review steps that fit a team your size.
  • Audit and lender prepSchedules, reconciliations and support files ready before they ask.
  • ERP and QuickBooks clean-upChart of accounts, old balances and messy history put right.

3 · FP&A (planning and analysis)

Know where the business is heading, not just where it's been.

Clean books tell you what happened. Planning tells you what's next: how much cash you'll have, which locations or jobs make money, and what an expansion would really cost.

  • Annual budgetBuilt with you, then compared to actuals every month.
  • Rolling forecastUpdated each month so the year-end number is never a surprise.
  • 13-week cash flowWeek by week, so you see a tight week before it arrives.
  • Profit by location, branch or jobSee which parts of the business carry the rest.
  • KPI dashboardsThe handful of numbers that matter for your business, in one place.
  • Board and bank reporting packagesThe same clear pack every month for your board, lender or investors.
  • Scenario planningWhat happens to profit and cash if you open a location, hire, or buy a company.

Who it's for: growing companies that want a plan to steer by, and owners preparing for expansion, a loan or an acquisition.
