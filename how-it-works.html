What happens to a transaction.

It arrives

From your bank, card, Square, Stripe, Zelle — or as a bill or receipt in your Gmail or Outlook inbox.

Aldeva recognizes it

Using your own rules, your industry and your history with that vendor. If it's routine, it's posted to your books with a note saying why.

Anything unusual comes to you

A new vendor, an amount that's out of pattern, a possible duplicate, a bill over your limit. Nothing is guessed.

You approve in a tap

Your answer teaches the system, so next time it posts by itself.

Month-end: matched, reviewed, signed

Accounts matched to statements, month-end entries booked, a CPA reviews and signs. Reports land in your inbox.

Beyond the books

Pay your bills in a few clicks

Get paid faster

Friendly email and text reminders with a pay-now link. Our AI assistant calls customers who are late.

Payroll, done in-house

We run your payroll ourselves: direct deposit, tax deposits from your own account, quarterly filings, W-2s. No separate payroll company.

Budget & 13-week cash

A simple budget and a week-by-week cash forecast, so you see what's coming before it hits.

Taxes, ready on time
