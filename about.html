About Aldeva

Finance on autopilot, signed by a CPA.

Aldeva Finance is a Florida accounting firm built around one idea: small and growing businesses shouldn't have to choose between doing the books themselves and paying for a full finance team.

We built software that posts every transaction into your own books, hands you only the few that need you, pays your bills in a few clicks and chases the customers who haven't paid. Then a CPA closes and signs your month — every month.

Built by people who have done this work for years — bookkeeping, month-end close, cash forecasting and tax — and who rebuilt it on today's tools instead of yesterday's spreadsheets.
